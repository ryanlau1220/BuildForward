# Golden demo scenario

Use the default **illustrative sample** to show one decision path across the two predictive features. The sample describes an assumed OOP record-management project, the next Software Testing module, and a simulated cohort. It is not a real student's submission or institutional cohort.

## Walkthrough

1. In **Project continuity**, keep **Software Testing · Semester 2** selected and run the comparison. The sample assumes the earlier project contains reusable object-oriented code, modular structure, persistence, and testable business logic. The trained classifier returns **CONTINUE** and the screen shows its evidence, the heuristic baseline, and the synthetic-rubric limitation. Explain that this demonstrates runtime inference; its 1.00 rubric holdout score is not real-world validation.
2. Open **Cohort outlook**. The first market-supported gap is **Unit Testing**. The simulated cohort starts at 18% coverage; one remaining module adds the illustrative 22-point gain, leaving a 30-point gap to the illustrative 70% target. ILMIA's selected role-profile sample lists JUnit or Software Testing for 2 of 6 profiles (33%). This is a role-profile coverage signal, not a share of Malaysian job ads.
3. The score is `0.30 × 0.333 × 0.5 = 0.050`, where `0.5` is the runway factor for one remaining module opportunity. It is an ordering aid, not a probability. Competencies without a mapped market signal remain labeled **Gap only**.
4. Leave the scenario assumption at **+18 percentage points** and recalculate. The illustrative graduation gap falls from 30% to 12%. Describe this as a hypothetical scenario, not an observed intervention effect.
5. Close with the educator decision: review whether a later assessment should deliberately extend and test the prior project. BuildForward presents evidence and a scenario; educators decide whether reuse is pedagogically appropriate.

## Keep the story accurate

- The programme, project evidence, and cohort values are illustrative or simulated.
- Continuity training labels are synthetic and generated from the same rubric used in evaluation.
- The ILMIA role-profile skill material cites Q1 2024 and samples six selected roles. See [market-data.md](market-data.md) for profiles, mappings, and limits.
- The 70% target, 22-point per-module coverage gain, and +18-point intervention gain are demo assumptions.
- Do not claim that CI/CD has 46% demand or that the market dataset measures all Malaysian software job advertisements.
